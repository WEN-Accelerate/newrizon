Transmission 06 · Pre-flight checks

Frequently asked questions

What is an Earthizen?+

A citizen of Earth first — someone who identifies with the planet before any nation, and uses technology, empathy, and purpose to act like it. The name comes from the founder's view from orbit: no borders, one home.

Who can join the programmes?+

Students aged 8–18 join through their schools or open events; schools partner directly for the curriculum and Community Labs; anyone can volunteer or mentor. See Programmes for each vertical's entry point.

Are contributions CSR-eligible?+

Yes. The foundation is a Section 8 company under the Companies Act, 2013, and contributions are eligible under Schedule VII — covering education, skill development, and environmental sustainability. [Confirm 80G details before publishing.]

How is the foundation governed?+

A Board of Trustees is the supreme governing body, advised by a Global Advisory Board of astronauts and policy leaders, with an executive management team running programmes. Annual impact reports and audited financials are published.

Where does the foundation operate?+

Headquartered in New Delhi with Phase 1 operations in Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Pune — expanding pan-India and to Singapore, UAE, and the UK in Phase 2, and 20+ country chapters by Phase 3.
